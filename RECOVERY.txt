Recovery and verified scope

Return to English and wait for stable operation BEFORE disabling/removing the
Chinese mod. The tested private workflow uses the same-version Windows installer
to withdraw the manifest, reads it back to verify removal, and then reboots while
English is selected. A full restart clears the RAM resource shadows.
For the tested device, hold ONLY the top power button for about ten seconds and
release when the restart logo appears. Do not add other buttons.

Before 0.2.3 installation, the previous 0.2.2 Chinese state was returned to English,
its manifest withdrawn, and all ten baseline resources verified after a full
restart. 0.2.3 has NOT subsequently been uninstalled on that device. Its install/
undo implementation is covered by the existing 32-operation host protocol test.
The new source builder is offline and does not install, uninstall, or reboot.

Observed platform issue: Mods disappeared in some prior sessions after repeated
platform initialization. English plus a top-power-only restart restored it.
Do not claim the localization repairs this. Browser-page refresh was not proven
to be the cause. Never use the Lyra installer page as a Wi-Fi keepalive method.

Public distribution should make the English-before-disable requirement clear.
The public Mods enable/disable/remove route for this exact release has not been
separately validated; the proven rollback is the private installer workflow above.
